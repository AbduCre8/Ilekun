Opening doors to careers in technology.

Ilekun (pronounced ee-leh-koon) helps people who are held back by cost earn recognized technology credentials and take their next step. We start with exam fees and plan to grow into study groups, training, and introductions to employers.

Join the waitlistOur mission
Mission

Ilekun helps people who cannot afford it earn recognized technology credentials, starting by paying certification exam fees, so they can prove their skills and compete for technology jobs.

Vision

In ten years, the cost of an exam no longer stops a capable person anywhere in the world from earning a recognized technology credential.

What we do

Now

Exam access

We help people anywhere in the world who cannot afford it get access to certification exams, starting by paying exam fees.

Next

Study and community

Study groups, training, and hackathons so people prepare together and build real skills.

Later

Introductions

Connections to employers who offer shadowing or short internships. We do not guarantee jobs.

Next and Later are plans, not promises. Each depends on funding, partners, and what the people we serve tell us they need.

Our first program

We are starting with the Salesforce Certified Administrator exam, a credential used in many technology roles. Our first group is 25 people, and we expect to add other credentials over time.

  • You are 18 or older, anywhere in the world.
  • Your monthly income is at or below twice your local legal minimum wage. [NEED DECISION: confirm threshold]
  • No employer or school is already paying for your exam.
  • Two reviewers score every application against the same published rules.
  • We pay the exam vendor directly. You never pay us.

Applying does not guarantee an award. We keep your information secure and delete application documents after 12 months.

About

Ilekun was started by Abdullahi Olalekan Abdulkareem, a Salesforce admin and engineer who has led a Trailblazer community group since 2022. We are a small team and volunteers, with no employees at launch.

What we promise

  • We publish our rules and treat everyone the same way.
  • We never promise jobs or passes, and we publish pass rates, including people who did not pass.
  • We publish what each voucher costs and how much goes to overhead.
  • People with a personal connection to an applicant do not decide that application.
  • We name recipients publicly only with their consent.
